# Use a four-destination shell with a task-first Board

Pacta will use four primary destinations: Board, National Focus Tree, Focus Chain, and My. Board is the first landing surface and combines the App-owned Task list with Focus Progress, National Focus status, and Recent Focus Activity; a separate To-do or data-dashboard destination is not part of the primary navigation. National Focus Tree remains a structural canvas with Card Library and daily-confirmation actions, while Focus Chain owns task selection and session setup. Elite and Regular are task-classification filters in a secondary task-picker rather than separate primary pages. A Focus Session then moves to a dedicated countdown surface whose normal completion occurs only at countdown zero; exception controls route through Precedent Rule or Failure Reason flows. This boundary keeps the first action visible, preserves the distinct tree and focus models, and keeps the product focused on its self-regulation workflows.

The four destinations and their responsibilities are accepted product requirements. Production layouts, visual styling, and component details remain to be designed.
